01 / CONTEXT

Start with the problem.

Imaging datasets contain more than raw pixel values. Researchers may want to compare derived image features, inspect distributions and understand which observations support a chart or an answer.

My contribution

Worked on image-data exploration workflows, model-output filtering, derived features and integration of analytics into an accessible interface.

04 / ENGINEERING REASONING

Decisions & trade-offs.

Why work from derived features?

Tabular measures make comparisons and statistical exploration easier than manually inspecting every image.

Why attach plots and evidence?

A conversational statement is easier to verify when paired with an inspectable chart or supporting numbers.

Where must caution be used?

Exploratory patterns can reflect dataset composition or model error and should not be interpreted as medical diagnoses.

05 / EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

What the work establishes.

REPORTED WORK & EVIDENCE

The source materials describe development-stage feature extraction, exploratory visualizations and test runs. They do not establish clinical effectiveness or validated diagnostic inference.

SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Exploratory data analysis only. Visual patterns, model scores and generated explanations are not clinical conclusions.
