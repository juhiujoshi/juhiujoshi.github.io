Hybrid patent retrieval.

Combining lexical recall with claim-level semantic relevance.

A two-stage approach for finding related patent documents using specification search for candidate discovery and claim-level semantic search for relevance ranking.

01 / CONTEXT

Start with the problem.

Patent research requires distinguishing documents that merely share keywords from those that discuss conceptually related inventions. Patent documents also have different text structures: long descriptive specifications and legally significant claims.

My contribution

Worked on parsing, index design, lexical and semantic retrieval experiments, evaluation and communication of results to stakeholders.

01

Structure-aware document ingestion

Patent XML was converted into records with separate specification and claim content. Dependent claims were associated with their parent claims when context was needed.

02

Two-stage retrieval

Lexical search identified candidate documents from long specification text; dense similarity then assessed claim-level relationships within the candidate set.

03

Relevance-oriented evaluation

Evaluation experiments considered ranking quality with information-retrieval measures, rather than judging the system solely by whether it returned a plausible-looking result.

04 / ENGINEERING REASONING

Decisions & trade-offs.

Why BM25 for specifications?

Long technical specifications contain discriminating terminology, while typical embedding models have context-length constraints.

Why compare claims semantically?

Claims can express similar concepts using different wording; semantic ranking can complement exact lexical matches.

What is the trade-off?

A second-stage ranker can only consider documents found by initial retrieval, so candidate recall remains important.

05 / EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

What the work establishes.

REPORTED WORK & EVIDENCE

The source summary describes early experiments using 123 queries against approximately 3,000 patents and later larger-scale data preparation. It does not establish a final validated accuracy for the scaled pipeline; dataset volumes and internal benchmarks are intentionally excluded.

SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Experimental retrieval workflow. Similarity is not a legal opinion on infringement, patentability or validity. Final relevance requires expert review.
